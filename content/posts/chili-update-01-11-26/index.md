## About the new look (the shell)

The shell was made with this *wild new technology* of *3D printing*. It consists of a front and back plate, which are secured together by a single screw – easy assembly and disassembly. PLA was used for the first prototype, but I’m considering trying different materials in the future.
A wild idea that has been brewing in mine and Kacper’s minds since we came up with the idea for this console, was to have a transparent shell. *Frutiger aero* my beloved…. So, wait up and we might surprise you with a new fresh 2000s style look.


## chilichip in the world
I was happy to show our prototype to many cool people around Warsaw and Copenhagen. I presented the console to many of my peers. I also managed to sneak it in a Unity student event. While our work is not fully in Unity's lane, I was happy to hear positive feedback from the game industry professionals.
