Hello world! We are **chilichip**, an indie electronics collective. We make
small, playful devices where **innovation meets play**.

## What we believe

- **Curiosity first.** Every circuit starts with a question: what would be fun to build?
- **Simple, like nature.** We keep our designs small and clear.
- **Open by default.** Our hardware and software are open source. You can read, build and change all of it.
- **Personal, not a fad.** We don't chase the next gadget trend. We want devices that feel thoughtful and a little bit spicy. 🌶️

## What comes next

This is just the start. We will share what we build here on the blog, step by
step: boards, games and the tools to make them.

Come along for the ride:

- Read [about us](/about/) and the team.
- Follow our code on [GitHub](https://github.com/chili-chip).
- Say hi on [Discord](https://discord.gg/xB9sPYKBZc).

See you soon! 🚀
