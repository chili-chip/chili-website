## Who we are

chilichip is an indie electronics company. We make open source software and hardware, and we like it a little bit spicy.

Right now we are building the **chilichip console**: a pocket game console that comes with a no-code game creator. You make a game in your browser on the [chili platform](https://platform.chilichip.eu), then play it on the console. The platform is also where you buy the hardware, get games and meet the community.

Everything we make is open source. The code, the boards and the docs are all on [GitHub](https://github.com/chili-chip).
