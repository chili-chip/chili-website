We are a small team, and we use generative AI tools. Our rule is simple: **AI is allowed, but what it made is tagged**, so you can always see it and choose for yourself.

## The tags

We use the same four kinds everywhere, on this site and on [Chili Platform](https://platform.chilichip.eu/help/ai-use):

| Tag | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Art** | Sprites, tiles, covers, pictures and other graphics |
| **Sound** | Music, sound effects, voices |
| **Text** | Stories, dialog, posts, descriptions |
| **Code** | Program code |

Spell check, autocomplete and plain editor tools do not count.

## On this site

- **Blog posts.** A post written with AI help says so under its title, for example *Written with AI help, edited by Kacper*. The person named there checked the post and stands behind it.
- **Code.** Much of our code, for this site, the platform and the creator, is written with an AI coding agent. It is reviewed and tested like any other code. Commits made with an agent are marked in our public history on [GitHub](https://github.com/chili-chip).

## On Chili Platform

Games on the platform are made by our community. Before a game is released, its creator says whether they used generative AI and for what. The game page shows the answer, as **No AI** or for example **Made with AI: art, text**, and you can filter the marketplace for games with no AI. A false "No AI" answer can get a game unlisted. The details are in [AI use labels](https://platform.chilichip.eu/help/ai-use).

## In the Chili Creator

You can connect an AI agent to the [Chili Creator](https://creator.chilichip.eu/get-started/ai/) and make a game by prompting. You never have to: every tool works by hand. When an agent changes a game, the creator tags what it changed, and that tag cannot be taken off when you release the game.

## What we do not say

We do not claim that chilichip is "made without AI". When something we make uses AI, we tag it.
