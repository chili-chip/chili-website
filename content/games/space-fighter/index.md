### How to play

- Pick **Play** on the title screen with **Z** (A). Arrow keys move through the menu.
- Fly with the arrow keys and hold **Z** (A) to fire.
- Clear wave after wave of alien formations. Every fifth wave a **boss** shows up.
- Shot-down enemies sometimes drop power-ups: weapon upgrade, rapid fire, shield, repair, extra life and bonus coins.
- Chain kills quickly for a combo multiplier, and finish a wave without getting hit for a bonus.
- **Esc** (MENU) pauses. Make the top five and you get to enter your initials.

| Key | Button |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys / WASD | D-pad |
| Z | A (fire / select) |
| X | B (back) |
| Esc | MENU (pause) |


### Options

- **Music** and **Sound** on or off.
- **Ship**: pick one of five ship colours. Settings and high scores are saved.

### About the game
**Ultra Hyper Space Super Fighters TURBO.** An arcade shooter for VGC Zero with endless waves of enemy formations, five enemy types, bosses with bullet patterns, four weapon levels, shields, a top-five high score table, explosions, particles and screen shake, and an original chiptune soundtrack synthesised live on the console's audio channels. Built with the chili-chip 32blit SDK for the handheld, desktop, and the web.
