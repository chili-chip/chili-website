### How to play

- Move the cursor around the 32×32 map with the arrow keys. The view scrolls when you reach the edge.
- Press **X** (B) to switch tools: road, residential, commercial and industrial zones, and the bulldozer.
- Press **Z** (A) to build on grass, or to clear a tile with the bulldozer. Each build costs money.
- Zones only grow when they touch a road. Homes need jobs, and shops and factories need people.
- Every month you collect taxes and pay road upkeep. Press **V** (Y) to see the city stats.

| Key | Button |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys / WASD | D-pad |
| Z | A (build) |
| X | B (next tool) |
| V | Y (stats) |

On mobile the embedded game is hidden; use the button to open it in a new tab.

### About the game
A small city builder in the spirit of the original SimCity, made for the 128×128 screen of VGC Zero. You start with $5000, build roads and residential, commercial and industrial zones, and a monthly simulation grows each zone through three building levels while the population and your budget change. Built with the chili-chip 32blit SDK for the handheld, desktop, and the web.
