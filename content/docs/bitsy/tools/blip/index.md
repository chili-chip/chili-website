The **blip** window makes short sound effects. Attach a blip to a sprite or item in the [paint](/docs/bitsy/tools/paint/) window, and it plays when the avatar interacts with it. Blips can also be used as notes in a [tune](/docs/bitsy/tools/tune/).

## Editing a blip

- The play button plays the blip.
- **blip-o-matic** generates a new sound from a category: pick up, greeting, bloop, bleep, magic, meow, random, or mutate (a variation on the current blip). The button next to it regenerates.
- **pitch**: higher or lower.
- **length**: how long it plays.
- **speed**: how quickly the pitch changes.

The bar at the top names the blip and has the usual **previous**, **next**, **add**, **duplicate**, **delete** and **find** buttons.
