The **record gif** window captures your game as an animated GIF, handy for sharing on the forum, Discord, or your marketplace page.

1. Start [play mode](/docs/bitsy/tools/play-mode/).
2. Press **start recording**, play the part you want to show, then press **stop recording**. The GIF takes a moment to encode.
3. Press **download gif** to save it.

**snapshot** saves a single picture of the current room instead. The button next to it switches the snapshot mode.

To set your game's cover picture on the platform, use **save image** in play mode instead.
