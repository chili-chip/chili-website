The **play** button, the green triangle at the top of the toolbar, runs your game inside the room window. The window's title bar turns red and reads **playing...**. Press **play** again to stop and go back to editing.

The Creator plays your game with **Citsy**, the engine that runs Bitsy games on VGC Zero, so what you see here is what the handheld shows.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | VGC Zero |
|---|---|---|
| Walk | Arrow keys or WASD | D-pad |
| Talk, advance dialog | Z | A |

## While you play

- **save image** appears in the toolbar. It saves the current frame as your game's cover picture on the platform.
- The **inventory** window updates live, so you can watch items and variables change.
- **record gif** can capture a GIF of what is on screen.

Play always starts from the beginning: the title, then the room the avatar starts in.
