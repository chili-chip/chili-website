The **tune** window writes background music. A tune is made of bars; each room can play one tune, picked in the room window's **tune** tab.

## Playing and bars

- **play** plays the tune from the selected bar, and **loop** repeats the current bar.
- **bar 1 / 4** shows which bar is selected. The arrows move the bar left or right, **+** adds an empty bar at the end, the copy button duplicates the selected bar to the end, and the bin removes it.

## compose

Click and drag in the grid to draw notes. Higher rows are higher notes.

- **note** draws notes.
- **blip** inserts the selected [blip](/docs/bitsy/tools/blip/) as a note.
- The strum menu sets how chords are played: off, up, down, or with a big or small interval.

## instrument

Choose the tone of the melody and harmony: tone A (square wave), tone B or tone C (thinner pulse waves), and the harmony's strum pattern.

## style

- **speed**: slow, medium, fast or turbo (60 to 160 bpm).
- **mood**: cheery (major key) or gloomy (minor key). Switching transposes the whole tune.

VGC Zero plays tunes on its two square-wave channels, so what you hear in the editor is close to the handheld.
