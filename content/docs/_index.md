## Chili platform

The [chili platform](https://platform.chilichip.eu) is where you buy hardware, buy and sell games, and keep your library. Its [help center](https://platform.chilichip.eu/help) answers questions about your account, the store, the marketplace, payouts, linking a console, and the legal texts.

- [Create an account](https://platform.chilichip.eu/help/create-an-account) and [link a console](https://platform.chilichip.eu/help/link-a-console).
- [Sell a game](https://platform.chilichip.eu/help/sell-a-game) and [get paid](https://platform.chilichip.eu/help/get-paid).
- [Legal and policies](https://platform.chilichip.eu/help): terms, privacy, refunds and tax. These answers are still being reviewed.

## Chili Creator

The [Chili Creator](https://creator.chilichip.eu/) is a collection of game editors in your browser. Make a game, release it on the [chili platform](https://platform.chilichip.eu) and play it on the console.

- [Get started](https://creator.chilichip.eu/get-started/): open the creator and find your way around.
- [Bitsy editor](https://creator.chilichip.eu/editors/bitsy/): rooms, sprites and dialog. Start with the [tutorial](https://creator.chilichip.eu/editors/bitsy/tutorial/).
- [Chili Beat editor](https://creator.chilichip.eu/editors/beat/): screens, things, rules and sounds. Start with the [Bee Home tutorial](https://creator.chilichip.eu/editors/beat/tutorial/).

## Console SDK

The [console SDK](https://sdk.chilichip.eu/) is the C++ SDK for the console, based on 32blit. Start here to code a game.

- [Getting started](https://sdk.chilichip.eu/getting-started/): install the tools and make your first game.
- [Tutorials](https://sdk.chilichip.eu/tutorials/): the game loop, input, sprites, sound, saves and a complete game.
- [Examples](https://sdk.chilichip.eu/examples/) and [guides](https://sdk.chilichip.eu/guides/assets/).
- [API reference](https://sdk.chilichip.eu/api/).

## Game SDK

The [game SDK](https://game-sdk.chilichip.eu/) is a library on top of the console SDK. It gives your game the parts every game needs, so you don't copy them from game to game.

- [Get started](https://game-sdk.chilichip.eu/getting-started/): add the SDK to your game. [game-template](https://github.com/chili-chip/game-template) already includes it.
- Modules: [input](https://game-sdk.chilichip.eu/input/), [menus](https://game-sdk.chilichip.eu/menu/), [screens](https://game-sdk.chilichip.eu/screens/), [scenes](https://game-sdk.chilichip.eu/scenes/), [saves and high scores](https://game-sdk.chilichip.eu/save/), [music and sound](https://game-sdk.chilichip.eu/audio/) and [effects](https://game-sdk.chilichip.eu/effects/).
- [The demo game](https://game-sdk.chilichip.eu/demo/): every module in one small game.

## chilichip CLI

The [chilichip CLI](https://cli.chilichip.eu/) packs assets, builds games and copies them to the console. The console SDK uses it.

## citsy

citsy runs Bitsy games natively on the console.

- [citsy player docs](https://citsy.chilichip.eu/docs/): play Bitsy games on the console or in the [web player](https://citsy.chilichip.eu/).
- [citsy engine docs](https://chili-chip.github.io/citsy/): the C++ engine, for developers.
